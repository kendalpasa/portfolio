Dungeon That Has Gems

A small 2D prototype inspired by Soul Knight. The player wakes up in a dungeon and must collect four energy gems scattered around the map. While navigating the dungeon, enemies can be knocked back using a knockback weapon. Once all the gems are placed on the central altar, the door opens and the level is completed. Created in Unity and C#.

2025 NASA Space Apps Challenge Embiggen Your Eyes! Prototype

Developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, this prototype allows users to explore high-resolution space images in detail. Built using OpenSeadragon and NASA APIs, the project demonstrates interactive image viewing and exploration techniques. Completed as part of a team project.

Saint Sinner UI Designe

The Saint Sinner is a third-person story puzzle game set in the dark, gothic atmosphere of the 1600s. The game's core concept revolves around "healing" the world. The character we control, "The Sinner," solves puzzles in various locations (church, mansion, forest, etc.) to find the fragments of the shattered Holy Sword. A key feature of the game is that initially the world is bleak, colorless, and silent, but as the player collects the sword fragments, the colors and sounds of the world begin to return.

These are visuals designs for the game's UI.
