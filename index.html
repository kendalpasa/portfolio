Dungeon That Has Gems

A small 2D prototype inspired by Soul Knight. The player wakes up in a dungeon and must collect four energy gems scattered around the map. While navigating the dungeon, enemies can be knocked back using a knockback weapon. Once all the gems are placed on the central altar, the door opens and the level is completed. Created in Unity and C#.
